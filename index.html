ORIGIN STORY

The Beginning.

Somewhere beyond the world we know exists a universe shaped by dreams.

Here, imagination is not just an idea. It has the power to create life.

From forgotten dreams, impossible creatures were born. From powerful visions came mystical beings. And from the deepest corners of imagination emerged cosmic entities unlike anything ever seen.

They are known as Dreamers.

No two Dreamers are the same. Each carries its own identity, abilities, and connection to the force that created it.

Nobody knows where the first dream came from.

And perhaps that mystery was never meant to be solved.
