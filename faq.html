Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DREAMVERSE, the Genesis Collection, the growing ecosystem, and the journey ahead.

About DREAMVERSE

What is DREAMVERSE?

DREAMVERSE is a creative digital universe built for people who dream, envision, create, and build toward the future.

It brings together original Dreamers, storytelling, community participation, creative tools, and an evolving ecosystem.

What are Dreamers?

Dreamers are unique digital identities within the DREAMVERSE universe. They can take the form of fantasy creatures, mystical beings, cosmic entities, abstract designs, and other unexpected creations.

Each Dreamer represents a place within the evolving DREAMVERSE universe.

What is the DREAMVERSE ecosystem?

DREAMVERSE is designed to grow beyond the Genesis Collection. The ecosystem is planned around several connected platforms and experiences.

  • DreamVerse Studio — Creative NFT tools
  • DreamVerse Stories — Lore and storytelling
  • DreamVerse Quest — Community quests and activities
  • DreamVerse Academy — Learning and Web3 knowledge
  • DreamVerse Creators — Creator opportunities
  • DreamVerse Marketplace — Future marketplace
  • DreamVerse Launchpad — Future launch platform
  • DreamVerse Identity — Future holder identity experience

Some of these platforms are still in development and may evolve as DREAMVERSE grows.

Genesis Collection

What is the Genesis Collection?

The Genesis Collection is the first collection of DREAMVERSE Dreamers.

With a maximum supply of 3,333 Dreamers, the Genesis Collection represents the starting point of the DREAMVERSE universe and its broader ecosystem.

What is the maximum supply?

The Genesis Collection has a fixed maximum supply of 3,333 Dreamers.

Where can I mint or purchase a Dreamer?

The DREAMVERSE Genesis Collection will be available through OpenSea, which serves as the primary platform for purchasing the Genesis NFTs.

Official availability and mint information will be communicated through DREAMVERSE's official channels.

DREAMVERSE Genesis Mint Policy

What are the Genesis mint details?

  • Maximum Supply: 3,333 NFTs
  • Mint Window: 30 Days
  • Treasury Allocation: $0.00
  • Team Allocation: $0.00
  • Waitlist Mint: $0.99
  • Public Mint: $1.33
  • Mint Platform: OpenSea
  • Mint Date: TBA

How long will the Genesis Mint remain open?

The Genesis Mint will remain open for 30 days from the official launch date.

What does Treasury Allocation mean?

Treasury Allocation refers to Genesis Dreamers reserved by DREAMVERSE for the project Treasury.

It is not a public free-mint tier.

What happens to unminted NFTs after 30 days?

After the 30-day mint window closes, any remaining unminted Genesis NFTs will be allocated to the DREAMVERSE Treasury.

Treasury-held NFTs may support future ecosystem milestones, community activities, collaborations, contests, and other initiatives that contribute to the development of DREAMVERSE.

Dreamer Ownership & Utility

What does holding a Dreamer provide?

Holding a Dreamer provides a place within the DREAMVERSE community and may provide access to holder-focused opportunities introduced through the ecosystem and roadmap.

These may include community participation, contests, voting, exclusive content, early access opportunities, and future ecosystem features.

Are all future utilities guaranteed?

No.

Future utilities, benefits, events, collaborations, platforms, and ecosystem features are subject to development and may change as DREAMVERSE evolves.

Does holding a Dreamer guarantee profit or financial returns?

No.

A DREAMVERSE Dreamer does not guarantee profits, rewards, airdrops, financial returns, or future value appreciation.

Roadmap & Future Development

What is DREAMVERSE building toward?

DREAMVERSE is designed to grow from its Genesis Collection into a broader creative and digital ecosystem.

What is planned for the ecosystem?

  • Phase 1 — The Dreamers: Genesis Collection, Studio, Community, Stories, Quests and Contests
  • Phase 2 — The Ecosystem: Quest, Academy, Creators, NFT Explorer, Web3 Knowledge and Community Activities
  • Phase 3 — Expansion: Marketplace, Launchpad, Creator Collaborations, Future Collections and Early Access Opportunities
  • Phase 4 — The Future: Dreamer Identity, Holder Dashboard, Wallet Verification, Points & Levels, Future Utilities and Expanded Ecosystem

Roadmap items represent planned development and are not guarantees of completion or availability by a specific date.

Our Commitment

Whether the Genesis Collection reaches full supply or not, DREAMVERSE will continue building according to its roadmap and evolving utility.

A complete mint is not the finish line. DREAMVERSE is built for the long term.
