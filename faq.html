Frequently Asked Questions

What is DREAMVERSE?

DREAMVERSE is a digital universe built for people who dream, envision, create, and build toward the future. It brings together original Dreamers, stories, community participation, and an evolving ecosystem.

What is the Genesis Collection?

The Genesis Collection is the first collection of DREAMVERSE Dreamers. Each Dreamer represents a unique identity within the DREAMVERSE universe and serves as an entry point into the growing ecosystem.

What is the maximum supply?

The Genesis Collection has a fixed maximum supply of 3,333 Dreamers.

How much does a Dreamer cost?

  • Treasury Allocation: $0.00
  • Waitlist Mint: $0.99
  • Public Mint: $1.33

Treasury allocation refers to Dreamers reserved by the DREAMVERSE project for its Treasury. It is not a public free-mint tier.

Where can I mint?

The DREAMVERSE Genesis Collection will be available through OpenSea, which serves as the primary platform for purchasing the NFTs.

How long is the Genesis Mint open?

The Genesis Mint will remain open for 30 days from the official launch date.

What happens to unminted NFTs after 30 days?

After the 30-day mint window closes, any remaining unminted Genesis NFTs will be transferred to the DREAMVERSE Treasury allocation.

These Treasury NFTs will be reserved for future ecosystem milestones, community activities, collaborations, contests, and other initiatives that support the development of DREAMVERSE. Their use will be communicated transparently to the community.

What does holding a Dreamer provide?

Holding a Dreamer provides a place within the DREAMVERSE community and access to holder-focused opportunities introduced through the ecosystem and roadmap, including community participation, contests, voting, and future utilities.

Utility Disclaimer: Future utilities, benefits, events, collaborations, and ecosystem features are subject to development and may change as DREAMVERSE evolves. Holding a Dreamer does not guarantee future profits, rewards, airdrops, or financial returns.
