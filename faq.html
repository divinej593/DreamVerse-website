Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DREAMVERSE, the Genesis Collection, the Dreamer experience, and the journey ahead.

About DREAMVERSE

What is DREAMVERSE?

DREAMVERSE is a creative digital universe built for people who dream, envision, create, and build toward the future.

It brings together unique Dreamers, storytelling, community participation, creative experiences, and an evolving creator ecosystem.

What are Dreamers?

Dreamers are unique digital identities within the DREAMVERSE universe.

They can take the form of fantasy creatures, mystical beings, cosmic entities, abstract creations, and other unexpected designs.

Every Dreamer represents something different.

What is the DREAMVERSE ecosystem?

DREAMVERSE is designed to grow beyond the Genesis Collection through identity, participation, storytelling, creativity, discovery, and community.

  • DreamVerse Studio — Creative tools for digital and NFT creation.
  • DreamVerse Quest — Quests, challenges, and community activities.
  • DREAMVERSE Creator Hub — The central creator ecosystem.
  • Dreamer Identity — A connected identity within DREAMVERSE.
  • Future Marketplace — A planned destination for discovering and interacting with ecosystem creations.

These experiences are developed progressively and may evolve over time.

Genesis Collection

What is the Genesis Collection?

The Genesis Collection is the first collection of DREAMVERSE Dreamers.

With a maximum supply of 3,333 Dreamers, it represents the beginning of the DREAMVERSE universe.

What is the maximum supply?

The Genesis Collection has a maximum supply of 3,333 Dreamers.

Where can I mint or purchase a Dreamer?

The DREAMVERSE Genesis Collection will be available through OpenSea, which serves as the primary platform for purchasing the Genesis NFTs.

Official mint availability and instructions will be announced through official DREAMVERSE channels.

Genesis Mint

What are the current Genesis mint details?

  • Maximum Supply: 3,333 NFTs

  • Waitlist Mint: 0.00034 ETH
  • FCFS Mint: 0.00041 ETH
  • Public Mint: 0.00052 ETH
  • Mint Platform: OpenSea

How long will the Genesis Mint remain open?

The Genesis Mint will remain open for 30 days from the official launch date.

What does Treasury Allocation mean?

Treasury Allocation refers to Genesis Dreamers reserved by DREAMVERSE for the project Treasury.

It is not a public free-mint tier.

What happens to unminted NFTs after 30 days?

After the 30-day mint window closes, any remaining unminted Genesis NFTs will be allocated to the DREAMVERSE Treasury.

Treasury-held NFTs may support future ecosystem milestones, community activities, collaborations, contests, creator initiatives, and other DREAMVERSE activities.

Dreamer Ownership & Experience

What does holding a Dreamer provide?

Holding a Dreamer provides a place within the DREAMVERSE community and may provide access to holder-focused experiences introduced through the ecosystem.

These may include participation, voting, contests, exclusive content, quests, achievements, holder verification, and future ecosystem experiences.

What is Dreamer Identity?

Dreamer Identity is the concept of a connected digital identity within DREAMVERSE.

It is intended to connect a Dreamer with participation, achievements, and future ecosystem experiences.

What are Dream Points?

Dream Points are planned as a way to recognize participation and activity within the DREAMVERSE ecosystem.

Point systems, eligibility, levels, and related benefits may evolve as development progresses.

Are future utilities guaranteed?

No.

Future utilities, features, events, collaborations, rewards, and ecosystem experiences may be modified, delayed, replaced, or discontinued.

Does holding a Dreamer guarantee profit?

No.

A DREAMVERSE Dreamer does not guarantee profit, financial returns, resale value, or future appreciation.

Creator Ecosystem

What is the DREAMVERSE Creator Hub?

The DREAMVERSE Creator Hub is planned as the central creator-focused space within the ecosystem.

It can bring together creator profiles, collaborations, creator tools, discovery, community opportunities, and other creator-focused experiences under one ecosystem.

What are Creator Profiles?

Creator Profiles are planned spaces where creators can showcase their identity, work, projects, and contributions within DREAMVERSE.

What are Creator Collaborations?

Creator Collaborations are planned opportunities for artists, builders, writers, designers, innovators, and other creators to work together within the DREAMVERSE ecosystem.

Where do Creator Tools fit?

Creator Tools are intended to live within or connect to the Creator Hub where appropriate.

DreamVerse Studio remains a dedicated creation-focused tool for building digital artwork, NFT collections, metadata, and other creative projects.

What does Discover mean?

Discover is planned as a way for users to explore creators, creations, collaborations, collections, and projects across the DREAMVERSE ecosystem.

Will DREAMVERSE have a marketplace?

A Future Marketplace is part of the longer-term creator ecosystem vision.

Its purpose is to provide a future destination for discovering and interacting with digital creations, collections, creators, and projects within the ecosystem.
