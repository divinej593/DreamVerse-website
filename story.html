CHAPTER 00 • ORIGIN

THE DREAM THAT WOKE

A forgotten beginning. A vanished sky. A voice that knew a name no one had ever spoken.

NEXT THE LOST DREAM COMING SOON
View chapter roadmap ↓

Somewhere beyond the world we know, there is a place where dreams do not disappear when you wake.

They remain.

They grow.

And sometimes...

they wake up.

No one in DREAMVERSE remembers the first dream.

Not the oldest Dreamers.

Not the strange beings that have crossed a thousand realms.

Not even the ones who claim to have been here since the beginning.

“
Never follow a dream that knows your name.

For ages, DREAMVERSE existed in silence.

Impossible worlds stretched beyond the horizon.

Creatures wandered through realms where the laws of reality changed from one step to the next.

Some Dreamers lived beneath endless skies.

Some moved through oceans of stars.

Others existed in places no ordinary mind could imagine.

Every Dreamer was different.

Every Dreamer carried an identity.

And yet, they all shared one unanswered question:

Where did we come from?

Then, one night, the sky disappeared.

Not dark.

Not cloudy.

Gone.

Every star vanished at once.

For the first time, every realm of DREAMVERSE looked upward...

and saw nothing.

THE HOLLOW • UNKNOWN TIME

Then a light appeared.

Far beyond the edge of the known realms.

It fell slowly through the darkness before striking a forgotten place called The Hollow.

The Dreamers watching from afar saw the ground shake.

Then silence.

No explosion.

No fire.

Nothing.

Until the light moved.

It was breathing.

Once.

Twice.

“
Then came a whisper.

So quiet that only one Dreamer heard it.

Its name.

The Dreamer froze.

Because it wasn't the name everyone knew.

It was the name no one had ever spoken.

Its true name.

The light cracked.

Something was looking out from inside.

The Dreamer stepped back.

The voice whispered again.

This time, it said:

THE VOICE
You were never the first.

The light disappeared.

The stars returned.

The Hollow went silent.

But beneath the ground...

something had awakened.

Something that remembered the beginning.

Something that had been waiting.

And now...

it was looking for the Dreamer who heard its name.
